## About

Gatsby Global is the holding company behind **JAMES**, a platform that lets a business issue, sell, trade and redeem physical and digital assets, with every transaction checked before it moves and settled on Avalanche.

## JAMES

Most assets that matter (art, luxury goods, tickets, in-game items, loyalty value) are tracked in systems that don't talk to each other and can't prove their own history. JAMES gives a business one place to run the whole life of an asset:

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Issue** | An asset is created with a permanent, verifiable record of its origin and authenticity. |
| **Sell** | A buyer pays and ownership transfers, with the seller, creator and platform fees split automatically. |
| **Trade** | Secondary sales follow the same rules, so provenance and creator royalties survive every resale. |
| **Redeem** | The holder exchanges the token for the real thing, and the record shows it. |

Every step is checked against the business's own rules (who may hold it, where it may go, what it costs) *before* anything moves.

### Built for the business, not the blockchain

- **Guided setup.** Tell JAMES what you're tokenizing and it proposes a stack and feature set to start from.
- **Switch features on and off.** Identity checks, screening, payments and more are modular, so a business picks its own vendors and policies.
- **Connect what you already use.** Bring in data from point-of-sale, shipping and storefront systems.
- **Provenance by default.** Every asset carries a log of who made it, who held it and what happened to it.
- **A modern, non-custodial wallet.** Customers hold their own keys, with recovery that doesn't depend on us.

### Where we're starting

Art: marketplaces and gallery networks, where authentication, sale, secondary and redemption are the daily work. The same engine runs luxury, gaming, ticketing and loyalty.

### Status

**Closed alpha on Avalanche testnet.** JAMES is not live, nothing is on mainnet, the contracts have not been audited, and no real money moves. Our code lives in private repositories.
